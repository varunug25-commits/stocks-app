# M11 — Monetization Validation

M11 is a future product study. No payment code, prices, checkout, entitlement service, or fake locked UI is implemented.

## Hypotheses to validate

Possible free access could include a bounded watchlist, daily brief, limited Ask usage, and basic Why It Moved. Possible Pro value could include larger watchlists, material alerts, thesis tracking, deeper validated history, higher Ask limits, and richer brief personalization.

The exact stock limit, usage limit, packaging, trial, and price must come from user research, provider cost/licensing review, and willingness-to-pay tests. Do not choose prices merely to fill a paywall.

## Readiness dependencies

- Auth and reliable entitlements.
- Store-compliant billing and server-side receipt verification.
- Restore purchases, grace periods, refunds, cancellation, regional tax, and family-account policy.
- Clear separation between free data rights and paid redistribution rights — **UNVERIFIED**.
- Cost model for market data, AI generation, storage, telemetry, and support.
- Honest upgrade surfaces shown only where a working paid capability exists.

Success means users pay for reduced research effort and trusted personalization, not decorative AI output.
